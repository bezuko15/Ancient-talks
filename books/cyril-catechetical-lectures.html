Catechetical Lectures

St Cyril of Jerusalem · trans. Edwin Hamilton Gifford (NPNF, 1894)

This work is presented in 24 parts. Start reading

Contents

  1. Procatechesis, or Prologue to the Catechetical Lectures of our Holy Father, Cyril, Archbishop of Jerusalem
  2. To those who are to be Enlightened, delivered extempore at Jerusalem, as an Introductory Lecture to…
  3. On Repentance and Remission of Sins, and Concerning the Adversary
  4. On Baptism
  5. On the Ten Points of Doctrine
  6. Of Faith
  7. Concerning the Unity of God.  On the Article, I Believe in One God.  Also Concerning Heresies
  8. The Father
  9. Almighty
  10. On the Words, Maker of Heaven and Earth, and of All Things Visible and Invisible
  11. On the Clause, and in One Lord Jesus Christ, with a Reading from the First Epistle to the Corinthians
  12. On the Words, the Only-Begotten Son of God, Begotten of the Father Very God Before All Ages, by Whom All…
  13. On the words Incarnate, and Made Man
  14. On the words, Crucified and Buried
  15. On the Words, And Rose Again from the Dead on the Third Day, and Ascended into the Heavens, and Sat on…
  16. On the Clause, And Shall Come in Glory to Judge the Quick and the Dead; Of Whose Kingdom There Shall Be No End
  17. On the Article, And in One Holy Ghost, the Comforter, Which Spake in the Prophets
  18. Continuation of the Discourse on the Holy Ghost
  19. On the Words, And in One Holy Catholic Church, and in the Resurrection of the Flesh, and the Life Everlasting
  20. First Lecture on the Mysteries
  21. On the Mysteries. II:  Of Baptism
  22. On the Mysteries. III:  On Chrism
  23. On the Mysteries. IV:  On the Body and Blood of Christ
  24. On the Mysteries. V:  On the Sacred Liturgy and Communion

Source & edition

Nicene and Post-Nicene Fathers, 2nd series, vol. 7, ed. Philip Schaff & Henry Wace (Christian Literature Publishing Co., 1894). Public domain (published before 1931).
