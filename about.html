About

Ancient Talk exists to reintroduce people to the Church Fathers and the great Christian writers who followed them, from St Ignatius and St Isaac the Syrian to G.K. Chesterton.

The heart of the project is the YouTube channel: unhurried voice-over readings of their words with quiet video in the background, so you can listen on a walk, a commute, or before sleep.

Every book in the library is in the public domain. Most can be read right here on the site or downloaded as EPUB and plain text, with each edition and source credited (Project Gutenberg, CCEL, Wikisource and the Internet Archive).

Read offline & install

Ancient Talk works like an app. Pages you open are kept on your device, so you can keep reading on a plane or with no signal. On any book page, tap Save this book for offline to keep the whole book.

To add it to your home screen: on iPhone or iPad, tap the Share button in Safari, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, open the browser menu (⋮) and choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
